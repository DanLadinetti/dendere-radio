Dendere Radio · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Versione italiana

Dendere Radio is an app for listening to internet radio, with a radio alarm clock that starts by itself. This page explains what data the app uses. In short: the developer does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts. The app is free thanks to small Google AdMob banners, which can be removed with a purchase (see below).

Data that stays on your devices

To work, the app saves on your iPhone or iPad:

  • favorite stations and podcasts, recently played stations, where you left off in episodes, and listening time;
  • alarm settings (station or podcast, time, days);
  • the profile you choose: name, avatar or a photo from your library;
  • app preferences (country, theme, icon) and a copy of station logos, to load them faster.

This data is never sent to the developer.

iCloud

If iCloud is on, the app keeps a copy in your own iCloud account (favorites, recents, followed podcasts and listening position, profile and preferences; not the alarm). It is only used to find them on your other devices or if you reinstall the app. This copy is managed by Apple and only you can see it: the developer cannot read it. You can turn it off in Settings › your name › iCloud.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

A small Google AdMob banner (Google Ireland Limited) appears on the app's pages. Never on the Alarm page, in the player or in car mode.

  • In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, on first launch Google shows a consent form (GDPR): you can accept, decline or choose. You can change your choices at any time in For You › Privacy › Change your ad choices.
  • iOS asks whether to allow tracking. Only if you say yes can Google use the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you more relevant ads; otherwise ads are not personalized.
  • To show and measure banners, Google processes data such as your IP address (and therefore your approximate location), device information, interactions with ads, and technical crash data. This data is received and handled by Google, not by the developer. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

"Ad-free" purchase

With a one-time in-app purchase (or a gift code) the banners disappear and the app no longer loads Google's library. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: the developer never receives your payment details.

Network connections

To show and play stations and podcasts, the app connects directly to:

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), the public station directory, to search for stations;
  • the servers of the stations you listen to, to receive the broadcast, and their websites, to find their logo;
  • Apple's iTunes Search, for podcasts and some logos.

As with any website, these services receive your device's IP address and the request made (for example the station to play). The developer receives none of this.

Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms: only used for the backup alarm, if the radio doesn't start by itself.
  • Photos: only if you choose a profile photo; the app only receives the image you pick.
  • Tracking: only for Google's personalized ads, if you allow it. You can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Children

The app is not specifically designed for children. The developer does not collect data from anyone, including children.
