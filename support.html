Frequently asked questions

How do I make the radio start by itself in the morning?
Open the Alarm page, choose the time and days and turn on "Alarm on". Then add the automatic start in Shortcuts just once, following the explanation in the app: at alarm time the radio starts without touching anything. You can change the time in the app whenever you like, without touching Shortcuts.

Can I wake up to a podcast?
Yes. In "Wake me with" choose "Podcast where you left off", to resume the episode you were listening to the night before, or "A podcast of your choice", to wake up to its newest episode.

What if the radio doesn't start?
The iOS backup alarm rings anyway, with a "Listen" button. Check that your device is connected to the internet and that the volume isn't at minimum.

I changed iPhone or reinstalled the app: will I find my favorites?
Yes, if you use the same iCloud account: favorites, recents, podcasts and listening position come back by themselves after a few seconds. You'll also find them on your iPad and Mac.

How do I remove the ads?
In For You tap "Remove ads": a one-time purchase, valid forever on all your devices and shareable with Family Sharing. On a new device tap "Restore purchases".

I received a gift code. How do I use it?
Open the link you received, or in the app go to For You › "Redeem a gift code" and type the code. The ads disappear for free.

A station won't play.
Sometimes the station's server is off or has changed address. Try another station with the same name in the search.

What data does the app use?
The developer collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
